Weekend Notes: Khichdi and Cricket

For a comforting khichdi, rinse equal parts rice and moong dal, then pressure cook with turmeric, salt, and a spoon of ghee. Finish with a tempering of cumin seeds and black pepper.

Cricket trivia: a standard over has six legal deliveries, and a Test match can last up to five days. The pitch is 22 yards long.

Garden tip: water tomato plants early in the morning, and stake them once they reach about thirty centimetres to keep the fruit off the soil.
